STEP 2: Format Change Karo (MOST IMPORTANT)
────────────────────────────────────────────
JPG/PNG → WebP format mein convert karo
WebP = same quality, 30-50% less size

HTML mein use karo:
  <picture>
    <source srcset="image.webp" type="image/webp">
    <img src="image.jpg" alt="Twin Shaft Mixer by Alfanio" loading="lazy">
  </picture>

STEP 3: Lazy Loading Add Karo
──────────────────────────────
Har img tag mein yeh add karo:
  loading="lazy"

Example:
  <img src="mixer.webp" alt="Industrial Mixer" loading="lazy" width="800" height="600">

STEP 4: Max Dimensions Set Karo
─────────────────────────────────
• Hero images   : max 1920×1080px
• Product images: max 800×600px
• Thumbnails    : max 400×300px

STEP 5: Vercel Image Optimization
───────────────────────────────────
