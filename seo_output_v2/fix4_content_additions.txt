--- SECTION 2: Products Overview (150 words) ---
Twin Shaft Mixer:
Our twin shaft mixers deliver intensive mixing action for concrete, mortar,
and industrial materials. Available in capacities from 500L to 4000L, these
machines ensure homogeneous mixing in less than 60 seconds. Ideal for
ready-mix concrete plants, precast factories, and infrastructure projects.

Planetary Mixer:
Alfanio's planetary mixers are engineered for precision mixing of specialty
concrete, ceramics, and chemical compounds. The counter-rotating mixing
action ensures thorough blending of even the most challenging materials.
Capacities range from 100L to 2000L.

Batch Plants:
Our fully automated concrete batch plants integrate seamlessly with your
production line. With PLC controls, real-time monitoring, and automatic
calibration, our batch plants maximize output while minimizing waste.
